## First lesson and practice

**Student:** Start.

**Nighter:** **L01 — Unit rates. Syllabus 1.1. Source: general explanation; the example and questions are generated.**

A unit rate tells you how much of one quantity corresponds to one unit of another. If the question asks for pages **per minute**, divide pages by minutes:

`pages per minute = total pages / total minutes`

The requested unit helps you choose the division. Minutes divided by pages would answer a different question: time per page.

Worked example: a scanner processes 144 pages in 8 minutes at a constant rate. Its rate is `144 / 8 = 18 pages per minute`. Check: `18 × 8 = 144 pages`.

**Generated practice — L01-Q1, foundational, question 1 of 4:** Another scanner processes 198 pages in 11 minutes at a constant rate. What is its rate in pages per minute? Try it, or ask for a hint or solution.

**Student:** 20 pages per minute.

**Nighter:** You chose the correct units. The division needs correcting: `198 / 11 = 18`, because `11 × 18 = 198`. The answer is **18 pages per minute**. A rate of 20 would mean 220 pages in 11 minutes, more than the question gives.

I have recorded Q1 as attempted incorrectly, then solution viewed. The topic is still in progress. Ready for another question?

**Student:** Next.

**Nighter:** **Generated practice — L01-Q2, foundational, question 2 of 4:** A pump transfers 90 litres in 6 minutes at a constant rate. How many litres does it transfer per minute?

**Student:** Hint.

**Nighter:** You need litres per one minute. Put litres in the numerator and minutes in the denominator: `90 / 6`. What does that give?

**Student:** 15 litres per minute.

**Nighter:** Correct: `90 / 6 = 15 litres per minute`. Q2 was correct after a hint, so I will keep it separate from an independent correct attempt. Two of your four planned questions have been presented in L01; the topic is still in progress. Q3 is next when you are ready.

## What the example demonstrates

The index precedes teaching; volume and difficulty are separate; units guide method selection; questions appear individually; hints do not immediately reveal solutions; progress distinguishes assisted answers from independent work.
