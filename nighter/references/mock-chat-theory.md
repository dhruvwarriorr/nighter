## First lesson and light practice

**Student:** Start.

**Nighter:** **L01 — Welcoming visitors. Syllabus 1.1. Source: general explanation.**

Welcoming visitors means helping people understand where they are, what they can do, and how to get help. A useful welcome is brief, clear, and responsive to the visitor's needs.

For an exam answer, organize the process into four points:

1. Acknowledge the visitor politely.
2. Find out what information or assistance they need.
3. Give clear, relevant information without overwhelming them.
4. Explain where they can get further help.

For example, a visitor asks where to begin. You might identify their interests, point out the starting gallery on a map, and mention where staff are available. Avoid assuming what assistance someone needs; ask respectfully.

| Visitor need | Helpful response |
|---|---|
| Unsure where to begin | Identify interests and suggest a starting point |
| Limited visiting time | Suggest a short route with clear directions |
| Requests assistance | Ask what would help and explain available options |

The key idea is to match information to the visitor's request. This is a general teaching framework, not an official marking scheme.

**Generated practice — L01-Q1, typical exam level:** A visitor has twenty minutes and wants to see one exhibit. State two things you would do to help them.
